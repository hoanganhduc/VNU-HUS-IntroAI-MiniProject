## Group self-check / Nhóm tự kiểm tra

Complete this checklist before creating the proposal commit. It is a group
self-assessment, not a teacher certification of quality, feasibility, or
correctness.

- [ ] We have one to five members from the same course, and all listed members agree.
- [ ] Only our founder accepted the Classroom50 assignment.
- [ ] We reviewed the worked example but did not copy its identities, topic, or text.
- [ ] We searched the correct class topic board for our exact repository URL and founder username.
- [ ] Our selected problem is stated precisely and is not knowingly identical to an earlier recorded problem.
- [ ] We chose and justified our own scope, method, resources, outputs, and level of ambition.
- [ ] We believe the work is feasible by the common final deadline and will adjust it responsibly when needed.
- [ ] We identified the external sources and dependencies currently expected.
- [ ] We considered relevant integrity, privacy, licensing, safety, and lawful-use issues.
- [ ] The proposal commit linked in the topic issue is the exact version agreed by every listed member.
